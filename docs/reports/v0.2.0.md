## 1. Objective
Build a trustworthy way to measure retrieval quality, and a lexical baseline that every later
retriever (dense, hybrid, reranked, agentic) must beat.

## 2. Data
UIT-ViQuAD 2.0 (Hugging Face mirror `taidng/UIT-ViQuAD2.0`), turned into passage retrieval:
every unique passage of every split is a document, every question is a query, and the gold document
is the passage the question was written about.

| Split | Questions | Answerable | Unanswerable | Passages used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 28,454 | 19,238 | 9,216 | 4,101 |
| validation | 3,814 | 2,653 | 1,161 | 557 |
| test | 7,301 | 7,301 | 0 | 1,241 |

Corpus: 5,317 unique passages from 184 articles; 582 passages are used by
questions in more than one split. License: not stated on the mirror; treated as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(listing for v1.0); data is downloaded at run time and never committed. Not fully verified.

## 3. Method
- Tokenisation: NFC normalisation, lowercase, `\w+` syllables; no stopwords, no word segmentation.
- BM25: k1 = 1.5, b = 0.75, Lucene idf, unique query terms, ties broken by corpus order.
  Parameters fixed before the run; nothing was tuned, so test is reported alongside validation.
- Metrics: recall@k, MRR@10, nDCG@10 (trec_eval conventions); 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over
  queries (2,000 resamples).

## 5. Error analysis (validation, answerable)
Rank of the gold passage: rank 1: 1,759 (66.3%); 2-5: 511 (19.3%); 6-10: 117 (4.4%);
11-100: 198 (7.5%); beyond 100 or no shared term: 68 (2.6%).

Questions missed at 10 are shorter on average (12.76 vs
14.59 syllables). This is descriptive only; no significance test and no
manual inspection of misses was done yet.

## 6. Threats to validity
1. **Test answerability labels**: the mirror flags 0 of 7,301 test questions as unanswerable.
   Retrieval needs only the question-passage link, so the test row is labelled "all questions".
2. **Easy benchmark**: 5,317 passages, and annotators wrote questions while reading the passage
   (high word overlap favours BM25). Not representative of open-domain search.
3. **Single gold passage**: other passages of the same article may answer too; recall is a lower bound.
4. **Shared passages across splits**: harmless here (nothing trained); a leakage risk for M3 fine-tuning.

## 7. Decisions and next steps
- M3: dense (Vietnamese SBERT + FAISS) and hybrid (reciprocal rank fusion); report on all test
  questions and on test questions whose passage never appears in train.
- Cheap ablations on validation only: word segmentation, k1/b tuning.
- Later: a distractor corpus to make scale and latency realistic.
