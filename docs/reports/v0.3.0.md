## 1. Objective
Test whether pre-trained dense bi-encoders, alone or fused with BM25, retrieve ViQuAD passages better
than the M2 BM25 baseline, under a protocol fixed before the run (issue #10).

## 2. Data
Same benchmark as M2: 5,317 passages, gold = source passage. Query sets: validation
answerable (2,653), validation unanswerable (1,161), test (7,301),
and the subset of test whose passage is used by no train question (5,181).
BM25 on validation reproduces the M2 numbers exactly (MRR@10 0.744, recall@10 0.900).

## 3. Method (fixed before running)
- Encoders (pre-registered, not fine-tuned):

| Model | License | Max tokens | Passages truncated | Encode passages (s) | Encode queries (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `keepitreal/vietnamese-sbert` | not stated | 256 | 22.0% | 73.3 | 24.1 |
| `bkai-foundation-models/vietnamese-bi-encoder` | Apache-2.0 | 256 | 9.2% | 66.8 | 23.9 |
| `intfloat/multilingual-e5-base` | MIT | 512 | 0.8% | 79.3 | 24.6 |

- Input handling: e5 uses `query: ` / `passage: ` prefixes; BKAI input is word-segmented with pyvi
  (model card requirement); vietnamese-sbert gets raw text (its card does not mention segmentation).
- Exact cosine search (FAISS `IndexFlatIP`), depth 100. Hybrid: RRF with k = 60, equal weights, BM25 and
  dense lists each truncated to 100.
- Selection: best dense and best hybrid by validation MRR@10. Test reported only for BM25 and the two
  selected systems. Paired bootstrap (2,000 resamples) of the per-question difference against BM25.

## 5. Error analysis (validation, answerable): who finds the gold passage in the top 10

| Dense model | both | only BM25 | only dense | neither |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vietnamese-sbert | 1,627 | 760 | 75 | 191 |
| BKAI bi-encoder | 1,951 | 436 | 148 | 118 |
| e5-base | 2,261 | 126 | 179 | 87 |

e5-base and BM25 fail on largely different questions (126 vs 179), which is why RRF gains even though
e5 alone barely moves MRR (ΔMRR +0.005, CI includes zero). Fusing BM25 with a much weaker
system (BKAI, vietnamese-sbert) lowers quality: equal-weight RRF rewards agreement, and a weak list adds
noise. No manual reading of individual failures was done yet.

## 6. Threats to validity
1. **vietnamese-sbert may have received the wrong input.** It truncates 22% of passages versus
   9% for BKAI at the same 256-token limit, which suggests a PhoBERT-style vocabulary that
   expects word-segmented text; it was trained with a similarity (not retrieval) loss per its card. Its
   low score is therefore not a verdict on the model. *Unverified*; an ablation with segmentation is
   proposed below (validation only).
   **Update (v0.3.1):** confirmed. With pyvi segmentation its validation MRR@10 rises from 0.420 to
   0.510 (paired +0.090 [+0.077, +0.103]); still well below BM25. See [v0.3.1.md](v0.3.1.md).
2. **BKAI domain shift:** trained on legal text and machine-translated MS MARCO / SQuAD v2; ViQuAD is
   Wikipedia. Truncation at 256 tokens also cuts 9% of passages.
3. **Possible contamination:** the training data of e5 and vietnamese-sbert is not fully documented here;
   that ViQuAD questions were never seen in their training cannot be confirmed.
4. **Split disagreement for e5 alone:** a tie on validation, a small win on test. The hybrid conclusion
   holds on every set; the "e5 alone vs BM25" conclusion does not, and is reported as inconclusive.
5. **Leakage check:** no model was trained, and the gains on test questions with passages unseen in train
   (+0.067 [+0.060, +0.074]) are as large as on all of test.
6. **Benchmark limits** from M2 still apply (small corpus, one gold passage, test answerability labels).

## 7. Decisions and next steps
- Use **BM25 + multilingual-e5-base (RRF)** as the retriever for the RAG and agent milestones.
- Validation-only ablations: vietnamese-sbert with pyvi segmentation; weighted RRF; RRF k.
- Fine-tuning an encoder on ViQuAD train is possible but must exclude passages shared with test,
  and report on the unseen-passage subset.
- M4: cross-encoder reranking on top of the hybrid (an English MS MARCO reranker vs a multilingual one).
