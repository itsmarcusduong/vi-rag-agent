## A1. Does word segmentation explain vietnamese-sbert's low score?

| Input (validation, answerable, n = 2,653) | Passages truncated | recall@10 | MRR@10 [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw text (as in M3) | 22.0% | 0.641 | 0.420 [0.404-0.437] |
| pyvi word-segmented | 9.2% | 0.741 | 0.510 [0.493-0.527] |

Paired difference (segmented - raw): ΔMRR@10 +0.090 [+0.077, +0.103], Δrecall@10 +0.099 [+0.084, +0.115].

**Finding.** Segmentation helps a lot, so the M3 input was indeed wrong for this model. With segmentation
its truncation rate becomes identical to the BKAI bi-encoder's (9.18%), which indicates the same
PhoBERT tokenizer. It still trails BM25 by a wide margin (validation MRR@10 0.510 vs 0.744), so the
input format explains roughly a quarter of the gap, not all of it. The M3 conclusion "both PhoBERT-based
encoders trail BM25" stands; the size of the gap reported for vietnamese-sbert in M3 was overstated.

## A2. Does tuning RRF beat the default (k = 60, equal weights) for BM25 + e5-base?

Validation MRR@10 over the pre-registered grid (bold: selected; italics: M3 default):

| k \ dense weight | 0.5 | 0.75 | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.796 | 0.802 | **0.805** | 0.800 | 0.795 |
| 20 | 0.797 | 0.800 | 0.802 | 0.797 | 0.795 |
| 40 | 0.796 | 0.799 | 0.799 | 0.794 | 0.793 |
| 60 | 0.796 | 0.798 | _0.798_ | 0.793 | 0.792 |
| 100 | 0.796 | 0.797 | 0.797 | 0.792 | 0.791 |

The whole grid spans 0.791 to 0.805: RRF is not sensitive to these settings. Selected:
k = 10, dense weight = 1.0 (equal weights, smaller k).

| Questions (n) | MRR@10 default | MRR@10 selected | ΔMRR@10 [95% CI] | Δrecall@10 [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| validation (2,653) | 0.798 | 0.805 | +0.007 [+0.005, +0.010] | +0.010 [+0.004, +0.016] |
| test (7,301) | 0.776 | 0.782 | +0.006 [+0.004, +0.007] | +0.009 [+0.005, +0.012] |
| test_unseen (5,181) | 0.766 | 0.771 | +0.005 [+0.004, +0.007] | +0.009 [+0.005, +0.013] |

The validation row is optimistic (the configuration was chosen on it). On test, evaluated once, the gain
is small but its interval excludes zero, so by the decision rule fixed in #18 **RRF k = 10 is adopted**
for later milestones. In absolute terms it is about half a point of MRR; it is a minor refinement, not a
new result.

## Consistency checks
- vietnamese-sbert raw and the default hybrid reproduce the M3 numbers exactly (validation MRR@10
  0.420 and 0.798; test 0.776) in a separate GPU run.

## Threats to validity
1. Choosing among 25 configurations on validation inflates the validation number; test was used once.
2. A1 used pyvi only; other segmenters (underthesea, VnCoreNLP) were not tried.
3. The vietnamese-sbert training data is undocumented; contamination still cannot be ruled out.

## Decisions
- Retriever for M4 onward: BM25 + multilingual-e5-base, RRF k = 10, equal weights.
- `ENCODERS["vietnamese-sbert"]` should use word segmentation (changed in this release).
- M4: decide on reranking (see the conversation after this release).
